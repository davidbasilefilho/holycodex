# Writing instructions

Define the receiver's goal, observable success conditions, authority, and necessary proof. Account for governing instructions and existing policy owners; add only what is missing, resolve conflicts at their source, and keep procedure only where it materially affects correctness. Review the effective contract for clarity and contradiction, and keep skill selection metadata distinct from procedure. Preserve attribution when adapting upstream material.
