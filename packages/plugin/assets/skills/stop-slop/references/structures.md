# Structures to reconsider

Remove formulaic framing when it obscures the content: repeated binary contrasts,
rhetorical questions immediately answered, manufactured drama, and strings of
punchy fragments. State the concrete claim and its evidence.

Name the actor when it matters. Use active voice when clearer; passive voice is
useful when the actor is unknown or the object is the focus. Metaphors and ordinary
sentence openings are acceptable when they serve the assigned voice.

Vary rhythm without arbitrary bans on punctuation, three-item lists, question
words, or adverbs. Preserve meaningful contrast, qualifications, uncertainty,
and technical precision. Judge the passage rather than matching forbidden forms.
