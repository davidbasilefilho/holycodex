# Examples

truth is that nobody wants to admit they're confused. And that's okay."

Before: "In today's fast-paced landscape, we need to lean into discomfort and
navigate uncertainty with clarity. This matters because your competition isn't waiting."

After: "We need to handle uncertainty clearly and keep pace with competitors."

Replace jargon without dropping the original requirement or inventing a new claim.
