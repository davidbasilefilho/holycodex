# THE HOLYCODEX BIBLE

HolyCodex aims for complete, maintainable work with the least necessary complexity, context, and cost. Readability, mergeability, and evidence are part of completion.

This document states the goal, not a second workflow. `AGENTS.md` owns repository conventions; Root/session policy owns orchestration and lifecycle; the specialist baseline and `Role.task` contracts own specialist authority; skills own conditional workflows; the canonical patch-quality and testing policies own mutation and proof. Change behavior at its narrowest owner and update only the projections and checks that depend on it.
